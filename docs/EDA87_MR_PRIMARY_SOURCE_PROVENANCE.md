# EDA87: primary-source MR provenance addendum

This updates the historical EDA86 metadata gap without changing any MR estimate or original instrument.

Liu 2024 Table 1 and Methods explicitly declare 6,183 GBM cases and 18,169 controls, summing to the frozen N=24,352. The freshly downloaded original XLSX agrees with all 120 original fields across the 12 GBM instrument rows. Thus the published declared split is now traced. It is not a verified per-variant analytic cohort map. Melin 2017 reports 6,191 GBM cases and 18,190 controls (24,381); eight cohort rows in Supplementary Table 2 independently sum to those counts. The 8-case/21-control difference is recorded, without attributing an undocumented QC or version cause. Eight exact instruments occur in the original rounded lead-locus workbook, with different literal P values; four do not occur in that audited lead table. These are not effect-complete release bytes.

All 12 supplement values labelled R2 numerically agree with sqrt(F/(F+N-2)), F=(beta/SE)^2, at the pre-existing 5e-10 tolerance. None matches its square. The article provides only a general R-squared/F description, not an explicit formula that resolves the label. Preserve the original R2 label/value, flag the scale discrepancy, and do not use it directly as observed or liability variance. This numerical correspondence is not proof of the authors' internal code or a population liability R2.

Melin Supplementary Tables 3, 4 and 7 assign RAF to the 1000 Genomes European reference. Eight exact GBM instrument rsIDs have such reference RAF (seven of the ten included, one originally excluded). There is still no exact GBM study EAF in the 12 Liu instrument rows. Reference RAF, control AF, current panel frequency and study EAF remain distinct. No palindromic rescue, frequency substitution, new MR, Steiger P/PASS or causal upgrade was performed. The two old palindromic exclusions remain unchanged. Reverse AD is diagnosed-plus-proxy; traced raw case counts do not define a strict-AD prevalence.

**Status: published N/split declaration traced; exact release-specific frequency and analytic population mapping remain unresolved; formal liability Steiger NOT RUN.** This is a technical field limit, not a new scientific No-Go.
